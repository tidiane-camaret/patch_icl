\subsection{Synthetic Task Generation}\label{sec:results-synth}

The baseline is the same configuration trained with
$p_\text{synth}{=}0.5$ and the synthetic-bank canvas. On four held-out
sources with no anatomical overlap with training (ISLES22, Shifts-MS,
ATLAS v2.0, GNC\_705 -- lesion targets on MRI, one Dixon), the baseline
checkpoint scores Dice 0.008--0.082. Widening the
shape generator's family mix or dosage on the synthetic-bank canvas
(\autoref{sec:methodology-synth}) left every source within noise of
baseline. The bank turned out to be built entirely from CT scans, while
every target source here is MRI -- a canvas-modality mismatch, not a
shape-diversity problem.

\paragraph{Canvas source.} A matched pair isolates
the fix: identical checkpoint, shape family (scatter\_field), dose, and
training budget, only the canvas source changes from the synthetic bank to a real
MRI training case from TotalSegmentator. Every source improves except ATLAS v2.0, a
single compact chronic lesion -- the only non-multi-focal target of the
four, plausibly outside what a multiplicity-targeted family like
scatter\_field can reach.

sources from stage 2's peak.

\paragraph{Cascade inference.} Resuming the stage-3 checkpoint
into coarse-to-fine cascade training (\autoref{sec:methodology-cascade})
and re-evaluating the same nine OOD sources tests whether the real-host
canvas helps under a different training and inference regime, not only
the single-level one it was found under.

Under cascade inference, the real-host canvas gains substantially on
MSD Hippocampus ($+0.215$) and NasalSeg ($+0.097$), loses on HU\_LWK1
($-0.028$) and Shifts-MS ($-0.012$), and changes Dice by about 0.01 or
less elsewhere. It does not close the gap to Medverse on either source
where Medverse leads. The largest single-level gain, on GNC\_705
(0.223, \autoref{tab:synth-realhost-ood}), does not carry over: under
cascade inference both models score below 0.04 there. A likely cause:
the coarse level's prediction on this source is dominated by an
intensity-brightness confound rather than real segmentation (its
qualitative failure mode is documented in
\texttt{docs/datasets/gnc\_kidney\_lesions.md}), so the imprecise coarse
centroid misdirects the fine level's region-restricted re-crop away from
the lesion entirely -- a real limitation of region restriction on small,
easily-mislocalized targets, not just a canvas-source effect.
