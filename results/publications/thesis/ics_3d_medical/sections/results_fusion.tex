\subsection{Image--Label Fusion}\label{sec:results-fusion}

\paragraph{Isolating the bi-axial attention effect.} The design choice
this axis makes is keeping image and label tokens as separate streams
that repeatedly cross-attend (the slot axis,
\autoref{sec:methodology-fusion}), rather than fusing them once. To
isolate its effect, the reference model is compared against a
single-axis control that removes the slot axis (\texttt{arch.dual\_axis=false}),
collapsing image and label evidence into one stream -- closer in spirit
to Medverse's early-concatenation design
(\autoref{sec:fundamentals-relatedwork}) -- while keeping everything
else in the recipe unchanged.

The bi-axial model reaches roughly $1.8\times$ the single-axis control's
Dice, at both seen and unseen classes, for about $1.6\times$ the GFLOPs.

\paragraph{Not yet an apples-to-apples ablation.} This is a first look,
not a controlled ablation: the two runs continue from different
upstream checkpoints (from separately-trained bi-axial and single-axis
lineages respectively) and are read at different points -- the bi-axial
run completed its full 51-epoch schedule, while the single-axis run had
reached only epoch 40 and was still improving each eval at last check.

for both, both trained to convergence, ideally $\geq 2$ seeds, before
this is cited as a settled effect size rather than a preliminary
