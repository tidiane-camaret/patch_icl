\subsection{Training Objective}\label{sec:methodology-objective}

Each cascade level is supervised independently at its own resolution with
a combined binary cross-entropy and soft Dice loss, summed over the $M$
levels:
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} = \sum_{\ell=1}^{M} \lambda_\ell \left( \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}^\ell + \mathcal{L}_{\text{Dice}}^\ell \right).
\end{equation}
%
Level weights $\lambda_\ell$ are uniform ($\lambda_\ell{=}1$). Because
the query prior is detached (\autoref{sec:methodology-cascade}), each
level's loss does not back-propagate through the coarser level's
prediction.
