\subsection{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:results-setup}

\paragraph{Training data and model scope.} All models are trained on
the training cases of TotalSegmentator, restricted to its training
classes; the remaining classes form the unseen split. Each results
section uses its own training setup. The fusion comparison
(\autoref{sec:results-fusion}) trains all three arms on the CT subset
only, at a single resolution level. The cascade experiments
(\autoref{sec:results-cascade}) train the reference configuration
(dual attention with query-prior initialization) on both CT and MRI.
The synthetic-task experiments (\autoref{sec:results-synth}) train an
extended configuration, which adds cross-level register carry, a
pooling token, and synthetic texture noise, also on CT and MRI.
Numbers from different sections are therefore not directly
comparable. In particular, TotalSeg MRI is a held-out modality only in
the fusion section. \todo{Resolve before submission: does the thesis report a single
``Ours'' configuration, or keep this split?}

\paragraph{Baselines.} Medverse~\cite{huMedverseUniversalModel2025a}
(released weights, and -- where noted -- finetuned on the same
TotalSegmentator split) is the only baseline evaluated empirically here.
\todo{No Iris baseline exists yet; the comparison in
\autoref{sec:fundamentals-relatedwork} remains architectural, not empirical.}

\paragraph{Metrics.} Accuracy is reported as Dice (macro, per-class then
averaged, unless stated per-sample) and Normalized Surface Dice (NSD).
Compute is reported as GFLOPs, wall-clock latency, and peak VRAM where
available; VRAM comes from a standalone $B{=}1,K{=}1$ inference benchmark
rather than the evaluation loop itself, so it should not be read as
representative of a real $K{>}1$ eval run.

\paragraph{Datasets.} \autoref{tab:ood-sources} lists every evaluation source used in this
thesis, grouped by relationship to the training vocabulary. TotalSegmentator (the
in-distribution source) and FLARE22 both reuse the training
vocabulary~\cite{wasserthalTotalSegmentatorRobustSegmentation2023}, splitting into a genuine
seen/unseen pair; the rest introduce a target, class, or scanner modality absent from
training entirely.
