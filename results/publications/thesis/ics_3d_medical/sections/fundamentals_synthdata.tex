\subsection{Synthetic Training Data}\label{sec:fundamentals-synthdata}

Domain randomization trains a model on procedurally varied synthetic data
so it does not overfit to the narrow appearance distribution of a single
acquisition protocol or training cohort. SynthSeg~\cite{billot2023synthseg} randomizes the appearance of whole images to make a model independent
of contrast. Synthetic tasks for in-context segmentation serve a different
purpose: they add new target classes, while the anatomy and appearance around
the target can remain realistic. We therefore compare a SynthSeg-style canvas
with painting synthetic shapes onto real scans (\autoref{sec:methodology-synth}).
