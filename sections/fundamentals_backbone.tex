\subsection{Volumetric Encoders and Attention}\label{sec:fundamentals-backbone}

\paragraph{Convolutional volumetric backbones.} 3D
U-Net~\cite{cciccek20163d} and its self-configuring descendant
nnU-Net~\cite{isensee2021nnu} encode a volume through a downsampling
encoder into a feature pyramid, then decode back to full resolution via a
mirrored upsampling path with skip connections carrying fine detail past
the bottleneck. Deeper encoder stages have coarser spatial extent and more
channels; shallower stages retain spatial detail at low channel count.
This split -- a coarse, semantically rich bottleneck feeding a
computationally light attention stack, and fine, spatially precise early
stages reserved for the decoder -- is exactly what the coarse/fine tap
split in \autoref{sec:methodology-overview} reuses. UNETR~\cite{hatamizadeh2022unetr}
is a prior example of combining a convolutional decoder with transformer
attention for 3D segmentation.

\paragraph{Attention.} Scaled dot-product attention~\cite{vaswani2017attention}
computes, for each query token, a weighted average of value vectors, with
weights given by the similarity between the query and each key:
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{Attn}(Q,K,V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d}}\right)V.
\end{equation}
%
Multi-head attention runs several such operations in parallel subspaces
and concatenates the results, letting different heads attend to different
relationships in the same input.

\paragraph{Position encoding.} Attention itself is permutation-invariant,
so position must be injected separately. Rotary position embedding
(RoPE)~\cite{su2021roformer} does this by rotating each query/key vector
by an angle proportional to its position before the dot product, so the
result depends only on the \emph{relative} position between the two
tokens rather than their absolute indices. In 3D, this is applied
axially: the head dimension is split into three chunks, one per spatial
axis, each rotated by an angle proportional to that axis's physical
coordinate (scaled by voxel spacing) -- letting attention between two
cells at different cascade resolutions still reflect their true physical
distance, not just their grid index.
