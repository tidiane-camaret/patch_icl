\subsection{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:results-setup}

\paragraph{Model scope.} Two configurations of the architecture are
reported in this chapter, kept explicitly separate. The reference
configuration (bi-axial attention, query-prior init, register carry gated
off) is the source of every Fusion and Cascade number below. An extended
configuration (adding cascade registers, a pooling token, and synthetic
texture noise on top of it) is the source of every Synthetic Task
Generation number. The two are trained independently and not directly
comparable to each other -- the extended configuration is read as a
promising newer result, not a replacement for the numbers reported above
it. \todo{Resolve before submission: does the thesis report a single
``Ours'' configuration, or keep this split?}

\paragraph{Baselines.} Medverse~\cite{huMedverseUniversalModel2025a}
(released weights, and -- where noted -- finetuned on the same
TotalSegmentator split) is the only baseline evaluated empirically here.
\todo{No Iris baseline exists yet; the comparison in
\autoref{sec:fundamentals-relatedwork} remains architectural, not empirical.}

\paragraph{Metrics.} Accuracy is reported as Dice (macro, per-class then
averaged, unless stated per-sample) and Normalized Surface Dice (NSD).
Compute is reported as GFLOPs, wall-clock latency, and peak VRAM where
available; VRAM comes from a standalone $B{=}1,K{=}1$ inference benchmark
rather than the evaluation loop itself, so it should not be read as
representative of a real $K{>}1$ eval run.

\paragraph{Datasets.} In-distribution evaluation uses
TotalSegmentator~\cite{wasserthalTotalSegmentatorRobustSegmentation2023}
(117 classes, CT). Generalization is tested on seven held-out sources
spanning CT and MRI and classes entirely outside the TotalSegmentator
vocabulary (\autoref{tab:ood-sources}).
