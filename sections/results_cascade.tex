\subsection{Coarse-to-Fine Cascade}\label{sec:results-cascade}

This section asks whether the coarse-to-fine cascade introduced in
\autoref{sec:methodology-cascade} helps in-distribution at all; how the
resulting design compares to Medverse's own coarse-to-fine mechanism, on
accuracy and compute; and which of the cascade's own mechanisms are
responsible for the in-distribution gain.

\paragraph{Coarse-to-fine cascading improves accuracy in-distribution.}
A spacing sweep from 4mm to 1.5mm crop resolution (117 classes, 3897
samples) raises macro Dice from \textbf{0.361 to 0.538} (+0.177). The
locator's containment of the target structure is already high at the
coarse level (0.922 mean, vs.\ an oracle ceiling of 0.970 -- a 0.048 gap)
and does not predict which classes gain the most at the fine level: once
a class is roughly localized, the remaining error is dominated by
segmentation quality at the fine crop, not by further localization
failure.

\paragraph{Ours vs.\ Medverse, seven datasets, accuracy and compute.}
``Ours'' here is the predicted-prior checkpoint (the best-performing
configuration of the query-prior ablation below); ``Medverse'' is
released weights, native autoregressive inference -- its own
coarse-to-fine mechanism -- with its refinement depth level-matched to
our own cascade ladder per source -- the fairer comparison, even though
matching that depth actually \emph{hurts} Medverse's own accuracy. Four
held-out sources (ISLES22, Shifts-MS, ATLAS v2.0, GNC\_705) are excluded
-- both models score too low there to be informative.
\autoref{tab:cascade-medverse} reports macro Dice and mean per-sample
latency for both. \todo{TotalSeg CT's Medverse row is not yet the

Ours wins 5/7 (TotalSeg CT, FLARE22, MSD Prostate, HU\_LWK1, TotalSeg
MRI); Medverse wins 2/7 (MSD Hippocampus, NasalSeg) -- both cases where
the target is a single, large, well-defined structure that suits
Medverse's full-FOV multi-resolution pyramid. Compute favors Ours by
roughly $14\times$ on
mean latency -- and this is \emph{after} level-matching Medverse's own
depth to ours, which costs Medverse accuracy too, so an unmatched
Medverse would lose by even more on both axes at once.
Medverse's own coarse-to-fine mechanism is separately prior-sensitive:
feeding its own imperfect coarse prediction forward as a query prior
hurts its harness cascade universally, across all 7 OOD sources tested,
sometimes catastrophically (MSD Hippocampus 0.691 $\to$ 0.050) --
compounding coarse-level error appears to be a property generic to
coarse-to-fine cascades, not specific to either architecture's design.

including FLARE22/NasalSeg): the prior-source gain helps 5/9 sources and
hurts 4/9 -- weaker than the clear in-distribution majority.
